Monthly Wildlife Review
July 2026 Birds & bats, recorded in the village

July's birdsong thinned as the breeding season wound down, but the warm, settled spell in the second week brought the month's busiest single day for detections, with activity dropping off sharply during the cooler, wetter run that followed. Bats followed a similar pattern after dark — the mildest, driest nights produced by far the heaviest passes, while the coldest night of the month saw barely a handful recorded. One new species joined the year list, a reminder that the garden is still worth watching closely even as the peak of the season passes.
