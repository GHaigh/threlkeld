Beneath Blencathra
Monthly Weather Review
March 2026 Recorded in the village · 54.63°N

March was a month of steady Lakeland weather rather than extremes. Most of its rain held off until the final week, when 18 millimetres fell in a single day and the Glenderamackin rose accordingly, bringing the month's total to just over 92 millimetres across seventeen wet days. Temperatures stayed modest — a warmest afternoon of 14 degrees set against a hard frost of minus three early on, with the fell tops keeping their snow into the second week. The winds were rarely still, gusting to 48 kilometres an hour at their strongest, though the valley was spared the worst of the equinoctial gales.
